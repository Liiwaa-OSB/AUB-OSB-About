Leadership

Meet the Dean

Dr. Yusuf Sidani is Professor of Leadership and Business Ethics. Before becoming dean, he served as OSB's associate dean for research and faculty development from 2018 and as interim dean from October 2020.

An AUB alumnus, he teaches and develops courses in business ethics, leadership, fraud prevention and control, strategy, and organizational behavior. His research also addresses gender and diversity in organizations. His book Business Ethics in the Middle East (Routledge, 2018) connects global ethical standards with local business contexts, and in 2015 he received the Abdul Hameed Shoman Award for Arab Researchers in values and professional ethics.

Our structure

Leadership and governance

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​The Suliman S. Olayan School of Business is managed by the dean, associate deans, track conveners, program directors, and the professional administrators of the school. The school is guided by the Middle East Advisory Board, a board composed of accomplished regional thought leaders and trendsetters in business. The Dean's Advisory Committee is an elected body as per AUB’s faculty bylaws.

OSB is organized into the functional equivalent of multi-disciplinary departments, referred to as “tracks.” While each track has its distinct identity and designated faculty members, a track is markedly different from a department; it allows for multiple faculty membership, offers fertile soil for cross-disciplinary synergy, and facilitates faculty cooperation.
