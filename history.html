More Than a Century of Business Education

OSB today

A regional leader with a global standard

More than 50 full-time faculty, about 350 bachelor's and 80 graduate degrees awarded each year, and close to 10,000 business graduates from AUB over the decades.

Our History

From a department to an independent school

  1. 1900

    Business education begins at AUB

    Business courses are offered within the Faculty of Arts and Sciences, first as a department and later as a semi-autonomous school.

  2. 2000September

    An independent school is born

    Marking a hundred years of business programs, AUB establishes its School of Business as the university's sixth faculty, with 13 full-time faculty.

  3. 2001

    New BBA and MBA curricula

    Both degrees are redesigned around leading trends in international business education.

  4. 2003June

    Named for Suliman S. Olayan

    In June, the school takes the name of the late Saudi businessman and AUB trustee whose family has long supported the university.

  5. 2004

    Executive MBA launches

    A program designed for senior corporate leaders in the region.

  6. 2009

    The Suliman S. Olayan Building opens

    OSB moves into a purpose-built home on campus, supported by the Olayan Group.

  7. 2021

    AUB's first fully online master's

    The Online MBA welcomes its first cohort.

  8. 2023

    Two shores of the Mediterranean

    AUB Mediterraneo opens in Pafos, Cyprus, with a Faculty of Business aligned to OSB's standards.

  9. 2025

    AACSB reaccreditation

    Reaccredited after a November 2024 peer review, keeping OSB among roughly 6% of business schools worldwide with AACSB.

  10. 2026

    EQUIS accreditation

    OSB becomes the first business school in Lebanon to earn EQUIS, joining AACSB as a second global accreditation.
