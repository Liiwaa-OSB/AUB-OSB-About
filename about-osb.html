About OSB · American University of Beirut

Transforming Business Thinking
in the MENA Region.

The Suliman S. Olayan School of Business builds on more than a century of business education at the American University of Beirut, bringing it into a modern, research-driven environment. It is the first school in Lebanon to hold both AACSB and EQUIS accreditations.

Vision and mission

Our Mission

Enable world-class business research, learning, and knowledge transfer from our authoritative anchor point within the MENA region.

Our Vision

Transform business thinking in the MENA region.

Who we are

A regional leader with a global standard

Founded as AUB's sixth faculty in 2000, OSB carries forward a tradition of business teaching that began on campus in 1900. Today the school educates students from across the region in rigorous programs that balance theory with practice, taught by faculty drawn from around the world.

Beyond the classroom, OSB works with the private and public sectors through research hubs and initiatives in business analytics, entrepreneurship, family business, corporate social responsibility, and executive education.

OSB's accredited programs also extend to the Faculty of Business at AUB Mediterraneo, AUB's twin campus in Pafos, Cyprus.
