As a well-known business school in the MENA region, embedded in one of the world’s top universities, we are honored to have been acknowledged in many ways.

Business education at AUB started in 1900 but remained under the auspices of the Faculty of Arts and Sciences until the university established an independent school of business as its sixth faculty in 2000.

The Suliman S. Olayan School of Business (OSB) provides rigorous undergraduate and graduate business education with uniquely crafted curricula that are balanced between theoretical and practical approaches.

OSB includes a number of knowledge centers and initiatives that encourage teaching and engagement with the private and public sectors. The school currently enrolls over 1,300 of the most select students in the region in its world-class BBA, MBA, Online MBA, Executive MBA, and specialized master’s degrees programs in human resources management, finance, and business analytics. In addition, the school also provides executive education programs to leading organizations throughout the region.
