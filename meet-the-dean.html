Welcome to the Suliman S. Olayan School of Business.

For more than a century, AUB has educated the people who build businesses and institutions across our region. OSB's role is to keep that promise current: to give our students an education that meets the highest international standards while staying rooted in the realities of the Middle East and North Africa.

The past few years have tested our community, and they have also shown what it is made of. Through economic crisis and regional upheaval, our faculty, staff, and students kept teaching, learning, and publishing. In 2025 we were reaccredited by AACSB, and in 2026 we became the first business school in Lebanon to earn EQUIS accreditation. Both are recognitions of people, not paperwork.

Our priorities are clear: outstanding learning, research that matters to the region, and ethical leadership. Whether you are a prospective student, a partner organization, a researcher, or one of our alumni, I invite you to be part of what comes next.

Yusuf Sidani Dean, Suliman S. Olayan School of Business

About Dean Sidani

Dr. Yusuf Sidani is Professor of Leadership and Business Ethics. Before becoming dean, he served as OSB's associate dean for research and faculty development from 2018 and as interim dean from October 2020.

An AUB alumnus, he teaches and develops courses in business ethics, leadership, fraud prevention and control, strategy, and organizational behavior. His research also addresses gender and diversity in organizations. His book Business Ethics in the Middle East (Routledge, 2018) connects global ethical standards with local business contexts, and in 2015 he received the Abdul Hameed Shoman Award for Arab Researchers in values and professional ethics.
